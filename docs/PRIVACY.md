# viewMD Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

viewMD is a Markdown viewer and editor for macOS. It is designed to keep your
documents private.

## What viewMD collects

**Nothing.** viewMD does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
information or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising
and no accounts.

## Your documents

Your documents stay on your Mac. viewMD reads and writes only the files you
open or save, and the folders you choose to grant access to (so images next to
a document can be shown). viewMD remembers those folder choices on your Mac
only.

## Network use

viewMD needs no network connection to work. The only network requests it makes
are to load images that a document itself links to on the web (for example,
`![logo](https://example.com/logo.png)`), the same as a web browser would.
Those requests go directly to the site named in the document.

## TestFlight

If you test viewMD through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports
and feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in
[Apple's TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/sren/terms.html).
